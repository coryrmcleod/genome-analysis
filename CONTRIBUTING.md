# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. Explain the observed behavior, expected behavior, app version and operating system. Use a small synthetic file that demonstrates the issue; never upload someone's actual genome, medical report, account credentials or saved personal analysis.

Run `npm test` and `node scripts/check-public-release.mjs` before submitting a change. Keep uncertainty and unavailable results visible. Missing VCF records must never be treated as reference genotypes. Do not reduce a published model to fit resource limits or substitute unverified variant identities.

For new reference data or research definitions, provide exact source URLs, retrieval dates, hashes, assembly/allele checks, publication context and explicit redistribution terms. A scientific paper or publicly reachable download alone is not a software/data license. Preserve third-party notices and citation requirements. Do not add the withheld score/reference snapshots or source-paper fixtures without completing their licensing and verification review.

Project-authored contributions are submitted under the repository's MIT license. Third-party material must be clearly identified with its actual terms and provenance.
